The idea

Three events.
One combined score.

Jopathlon pairs a triathlon with a round of golf, then adds beer to the tally. Each part contributes to the final result.

  1. 01

    Endurance

    Do a triathlon

    Swim, bike, run—and record your finish time.

  2. 02

    On the course

    Play a round

    Your golf score adds to or subtracts from your triathlon time.

  3. 03

    The refreshment

    Count the beers

    The beer tally also adds to or subtracts from your triathlon time.

Put the three parts together to get your Jopathlon result.
