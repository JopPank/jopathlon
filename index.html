The idea

Three events.
One combined score.

Your official triathlon time is adjusted by your Stableford golf points and the number of beers you enjoy during the day.

  1. 01

    Endurance

    Do a triathlon

    Swim, bike, run—and record your finish time.

  2. 02

    On the course

    Play a round

    Start from 36 Stableford points: each point below adds 2 minutes, and each point above subtracts 2 minutes.

  3. 03

    The refreshment

    Count the beers

    Subtract 15 minutes for each standard beer. Your adjusted time can be calculated below.

Final time = triathlon time + golf adjustment − beer bonus.
